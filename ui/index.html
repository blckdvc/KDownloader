              Links

              Network

              Needed to seed: peers connect to you on this port. Takes effect after you restart KDownloader. If your router blocks UPnP, forward this port (TCP + UDP) to this Mac. µTP adds UDP peers but may lose packets at high speed on macOS. Bind to interface: if you run a VPN/Private Relay (many utun interfaces), UPnP discovery can fail — set your LAN interface (e.g. en0) to force it through your router.

              Peers and privacy

              Take effect after you restart KDownloader. The proxy carries connections to peers, not to trackers or the DHT. Peers in the block list can't connect either way. Private torrents never get the extra trackers. 0 peers keeps the default. Encryption hides BitTorrent traffic from providers that slow it down; it doesn't hide your IP address. Required loses the peers that can't encrypt.
